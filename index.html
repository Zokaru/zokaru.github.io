Services

How can I help you

User Experience Design (UX)

I always take a holistic approach to UX and product design: I act not only as a designer, but also as a business analyst, a researcher and marketer.

Furthermore, I place accessibility in a special place: I have a passion for the intricacies involved in designing (and developing) accessible products. Accessibility is not a buzzword, nor a plus: it is the very essence of a successful product.

Seeing how the final product can have an actual impact, both by improving (even a tiny bit) the user’s daily life, or by teaching clients about the value of UX, is what makes all the hard work worthwhile.

Web Design & Development

In a world full of templates and AI-made websites, building a custom, hand-crafted experience for your customers is the simplest way to show them you actually care.

I believe the importance in being fully present in the whole process of web development: from the first draft to the final line of code.

I've experience designing and shipping the whole spectrum of websites, from simple landing pages to full corporate websites, for global brands like Mercedes-Benz, Sherwin-Williams and Fairfax Financial companies.

Branding & Graphic Design

I approach branding is a discipline that takes time, precision and serious dedication.

It's about building a relationship, understanding what the business and the people behind it are really about, and representing that not only visually, but with ideas, voices and textures.

This is when graphic design for print takes the lead: in a digital-first world, physical interactions are not to be relegated. On the contrary, we must take care of them more than ever.

Illustration & Photography

I channel my life-long passion for drawing to breathe new life into your product or brand using vector, minimalist, and pixel art illustrations.

The power of illustration is not to be understimated, nor should the value of good, professional photography.

Video Editing & Motion Graphics

The need for an eye-catching ad, a captivating introduction for a website, or a memorable business presentation makes video an essential asset.

I use video and animation as part of my toolkit, as an enhancer of any design piece. Ultimately, the goal is to make your vision come to life, and what better way to do that?
